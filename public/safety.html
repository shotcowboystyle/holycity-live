Arrests and stops measure police activity, not crime rates, and are recorded where they happened, not where people live — so per-resident rates are skewed in commercial and nightlife areas. Rates are hidden for subdivisions under 200 residents. Only date, location, charge/reason and patrol team are requested; personal fields in the source aren't used. The City's "Incidents" layer stops in March 2017 and is left out. Stop reason codes are CPD's own and aren't explained in the source.
